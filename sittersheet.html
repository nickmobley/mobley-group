Who it's for — and the problem it solves

Who it's for: babysitters, nannies, and pet sitters who want a clean record of every sitting and what it paid — without a subscription, an account, or a spreadsheet.

The problem: sitting records end up scattered across notes apps, texts, and memory. At payment time — or tax time — reconstructing hours and rates is guesswork. SitterSheet keeps the log and the math in one place, on your device, and turns it into a tidy report.

Local-first, stated precisely

SitterSheet is described as local-first, and the entered data backs that up: the sittings, notes, and pay records you type in stay on your device — there is no SitterSheet cloud copy of them, and nothing in the product sends your records to us.

One thing worth knowing, stated plainly: the Android build initializes Firebase Analytics, so a strict "no servers anywhere" reading would be looser than the shipped behavior. Analytics is anonymous usage measurement — it is not your sitting records, which stay on-device. If that distinction matters to you, it should; that's why it's written here and not buried.

Who it's for — and the limits

  • For: babysitters, nannies, and pet sitters who keep their records in a notes app, a group chat, or their head — and want the pay math and the report done for them.
  • Not: an agency platform. SitterSheet doesn't find you clients, process payments, or sync a team — it keeps one sitter's records straight.
  • The preview's limit: this page demonstrates the core math and the log → report flow. The complete released application is what the Gumroad purchase delivers.
  • Compatibility: SitterSheet runs as an Android app and as a self-contained web build that works in a modern browser — no account to create and nothing to install for the web version.
  • Your records, your backup: because records live on your device, exporting a report is also your backup. If a device is lost before you export, its log goes with it — the app makes no cloud copy.
  • Pay math, not tax filing: SitterSheet totals hours and pay for your own records. It does not calculate taxes or withholdings and files nothing — household-employer tax questions belong with a tax professional.
