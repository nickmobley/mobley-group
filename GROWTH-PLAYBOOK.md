## Four-week launch checklist (not an autonomous posting schedule)
- Week 1: Confirm search property ownership; connect GSC/GA4/Business Profile; verify NAP and local business profile eligibility.
- Week 2: Publish one useful guide and 2–3 distinct posts that point to it; respond to real inbound inquiries.
- Week 3: Share one labeled product demo with precise workflow limits; invite scoped conversations.
- Week 4: Review actual GSC impressions/clicks, GA4 source traffic if configured, and qualified inquiries (with consent); prioritize next changes.

## Measurement definitions
- Impressions and click-through: Search Console, after user-authorized access.
- Search discovery: Google Search Console queries/pages, not guessed from ranking checkers.
- Contact click: optional on-site analytics event, only after selecting a privacy-compatible provider and updating disclosures.
- Qualified inquiry: request from someone expressing a concrete problem, budget or next step; use CRM/inbox with access controls.
- Leads, revenue and ROI: count only verified, attributable, non-duplicated records. Do not claim baseline values in advance.

## Operational safeguards
- Never create fictitious reviews, testimonials, case studies, geographic locations, statistics or results.
- Don't purchase ads or subscribe to paid services without explicit approval.
- Do not publish to connected social accounts without confirming the exact post, account, audience and timing.
- Business Profile address must follow Google's eligibility rules. Avoid displaying a residential address if clients are not served there.
- Maintain a verified business contact route and match it across site and local listings.
- Consent, privacy rules and escalation are required for automated calls/texts; website copy is not legal compliance assurance.
- Don't presume an automation or generic form creates a qualified lead without proof it delivers to a working inbox.

## Human-approval gates
Connect search and analytics accounts via approved access; choose a measurement platform; verify a customer-facing contact process; approve campaign publishing; approve any spending. The website remains usable without these integrations.
