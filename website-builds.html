Who it's for — and the problem it solves

Who it's for: businesses whose current site doesn't reflect the quality of their work — or whose first site needs to be built properly from the start.

The problem: visitors judge a business in seconds. An out-of-date or confusing site quietly sends them elsewhere, and every claim on it still has to be true. A build gives you a fast, clear site you own outright — your domain, your hosting, your content decisions.

What a build includes

  • A written scope: the pages, the structure, and what is not included — priced before work begins
  • Design in your brand, built mobile-first (your visitors are on phones)
  • The site on your domain, in accounts you control — never held hostage by ours
  • The essentials done properly: contact paths that work, basic search setup, an honest catalog of what you offer
  • Handover documentation: what was built, where it lives, how to change it

Support and limitations

  • Design is not traffic. A clear site makes the next step easy; it doesn't manufacture visitors, and we won't promise rankings or conversion numbers.
  • Your content decisions are yours. Wording about your business — its claims, its prices, its story — is approved by you before it publishes.
  • Revisions are bounded, in writing. Your scope includes a set number of revision rounds per page. Rounds beyond it are quoted before the work — never billed silently.
  • Accessibility scope. Builds follow our standing standard: semantic structure, contrast-checked colors, visible keyboard focus, and layouts that hold up on phones and at enlarged text sizes. A formal accessibility audit against a named standard is a separate scope.
  • Priced by scope. A one-page presence and a full catalog site are different builds; the scope says which one you're getting, in writing.

What you'll need to launch

  • A domain name (required). Registered in your name, paid by you to your registrar — typically a modest yearly fee.
  • Hosting (required). A hosting account in your name. Some builds run on no-cost static hosting; your scope names the host and any cost before work begins.
  • Your content (required). The words and images about your business, approved by you — we can help shape them, and nothing publishes without your approval.
  • Optional integrations (scope-dependent). Booking, forms, payments, or an assistant like ours — each named in the scope with any third-party cost stated.

“No surprise platform costs. Before work begins, we'll explain any required third-party accounts, subscriptions, usage charges, or publishing fees. Where practical, these accounts are established in your name so you retain control.”

Pricing and ongoing costs

Builds are priced by written scope — pages, features, and integrations named before work begins.

Ongoing costs are yours and small: domain renewal and any hosting fee, paid by you to those providers. Optional ongoing maintenance from us is quoted separately, in writing.

Ownership, handover, and support

  • Yours: the domain, the hosting account, the site files, and the content. We build in your accounts, so there is nothing to transfer at the end — it was never held anywhere else.
  • Handover includes: the live site, its source files, and a short guide to routine updates (text, images, hours) so you're not dependent on us for small changes.
  • Scope and changes: work beyond the written scope is quoted and approved before it is done.
  • Support. If something we delivered doesn't do what the written scope says, we fix it — that's a defect, not a new request. Anything beyond the scope is quoted in writing before work begins.
  • If we part ways: your site keeps running on your domain and hosting. Licensed fonts or stock assets stay under their own licenses, which the handover guide lists.
