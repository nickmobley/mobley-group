06 / Useful before you buy

Start with a better question.

Before buying an AI receptionist or another automation, make sure you understand what happens to your calls today, who follows up, and where requests get lost.

Read the missed-call field guide ↗

Three things to check first

  1. How many legitimate calls go unanswered during your busiest hours?
  2. How quickly does somebody follow up, and how is that recorded?
  3. Does your current phone setup permit the response and consent rules you need?

No assumed customer-loss figures. Start with what your own business can verify.
