Personal privacy · Written scope

Reduce the personal information people can find about you.

We find exposed listings, submit eligible removal requests, and show you what changed. The work is documented; the outcome depends on each site or search provider.

A search for your name, phone number, or address can surface people-search listings you did not create. Digital footprint cleanup is a guided removal effort with a defined list of URLs and a visible result for each one.

What the service does

01 · FIND

Inventory exposures

Agree on the names and details to look for, record public source URLs, and prioritize the listings you want addressed. We work only with your authorization.

02 · REQUEST

Submit eligible requests

Use available broker opt-outs, source-site removal paths, and search-provider processes. Some requests need identity verification that you control through the provider's secure process.

03 · VERIFY

Report the result

Log submission dates and statuses, distinguish approved from pending or denied, check what is still visible, and give you a recheck plan for listings that can return.

Clear limits, no “wipe the internet” promise

  • Removing a Google Search result does not erase the page from the website hosting it.
  • Some public records and other pages may not qualify for removal. Broker listings can reappear later.
  • We report requests and observed results separately. A submitted request is not a confirmed removal.

You receive

  • An agreed exposure and source-URL inventory
  • Eligible requests with dates and confirmation details
  • A status log: submitted, approved, denied, or still visible
  • One follow-up check and a recheck plan

Common questions

Can you remove everything about me from the internet?

No. The work covers agreed URLs and eligible requests; public records, other copies, and later republication can remain.

If Google removes a result, is the information gone?

No. Google says the source page may still contain it. We distinguish search-result removal from a source-site change.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. The FTC explains how to opt out of people-search sites, and Google explains its personal-information removal requests. Our service does the agreed tracking and eligible requests with your authorization.
